# Caption-pool refresh proposal — 2026-05-28

Derived from the website-scrape findings in `website-scrape-2026-05-28.md`. Six new **V4** variants — one per audience — engineered against specific competitor positioning gaps. **Proposal, not merged.** Approve any subset before adding to `captions.md`.

## Why these exist

Five signals from the scrape drove the picks:

1. **Speed dominates the AI-planner category** (Layla, Mindtrip, iplan, GuideGeek, TripPlanner all lead with "minutes" / "seconds" / "saves you hours"). GeKnee's current captions hit speed on A1 + A2 but NOT on A3, A4, A5, A6. Closing that gap on A3 (group friction → instant) and A4 (nomad logistics → instant) is upside.
2. **Layla absorbed Roam Around** — they're consolidating the category. A direct positioning beat ("while Layla types it out, GeKnee spins it") puts us against the segment leader without naming them in hashtags.
3. **No competitor leads with 3D globe UX.** A2 should LEAN HARDER on this visual differentiator rather than the generic "AI plans your trip" frame.
4. **No competitor has monument collection / gamification.** A5 should LEAN HARDER on the verification angle ("we know you actually went") — that's a moat.
5. **TripPlanner.ai uses "Smarter than endless tabs"** — same pain frame as our A1 V1. Already covered; no change needed there.

## Proposed V4 variants

### A1 — Overwhelmed Planner (V4 candidate)

```
"AI trip planner" is a category. GeKnee is a 3D globe.

Spin Earth. Tap a city. The Genie writes the days — places, times, the reason for each stop. Free.

→ geknee.com

#travelplanning #aitripplanner #tripplanner #travelapp #aiitinerary
```

**Why:** Positions GeKnee as in-but-different from the category. Globe-first wedge stated upfront. Keeps "free" + short CTA. New hashtag `#aiitinerary` captures search intent the existing pool misses.

### A2 — Spontaneous Explorer (V4 candidate)

```
While the other AI planners ask you to type, GeKnee asks you to spin. ✦

Tap any point on Earth. Get a real trip — when to go, what's beautiful, what you'd actually do. The globe is the planner.

geknee.com

#travelinspo #wanderlust #aitravelplanner #travelapp #wheretotravel
```

**Why:** Beats the segment leader (Layla "Plan my trip" → text input) without naming. Reframes the globe from "fun UI" to "the planner itself." Drops the "spontaneous" framing slightly in favor of "the choice IS the input."

### A3 — Group Organizer (V4 candidate)

```
Group trip planned in the time it takes to send the first WhatsApp.

GeKnee turns "where should we go?" into a shared trip everyone can see — vote on activities, swap stops, no 400-message thread. Plan in minutes, not weeks.

→ geknee.com

#grouptravel #travelplanning #travelwithfriends #aitripplanner #grouptrip
```

**Why:** Adds the speed frame that A3 was missing. Concrete time benchmark ("minutes, not weeks") matches the category's pace.

### A4 — Frequent Flyer / Nomad (V4 candidate)

```
The boring layer, automated. ✦

Flights, FX, visas, daily plan — under one trip. GeKnee handles the logistics so you do the parts only you can do. Built for people who travel as a job.

geknee.com

#digitalnomad #nomadlife #travelhacks #remotework #frequentflyer
```

**Why:** Cleaner positioning vs Hopper / Going / Skyscanner (each is one vertical — flights only, or hotels only). GeKnee's pitch is "the whole logistics layer, one tool." That's underleveraged in current A4 V1-V3.

### A5 — Gamified Status Traveler (V4 candidate)

```
Every other "AI travel planner" thinks it's done when you book.

GeKnee starts then. Stand under a monument. Phone verifies you're there. Badge unlocks. Rarity tiers from Bronze to Diamond. The only travel app that knows you actually went.

geknee.com

#travelgoals #bucketlist #travel #travelapp #wanderlust
```

**Why:** Loudest moat-positioning of the six. Names the category gap explicitly ("the only one that knows you actually went"). Matches the geknee.com home page voice ("GO THERE. PROVE IT."). Should be the V4 we test hardest.

### A6 — On-Trip Traveler (V4 candidate)

```
Trip planning is the boring half. GeKnee runs the trip.

Live mode: which museum closes in 22 minutes, where the queue is thin, when the light is best. Quiet, ad-free, on the ground. A real companion.

→ geknee.com

#travel #livetravel #travelhacks #cityguide #travelapp #citytravel
```

**Why:** Frames the entire AI-planner segment as half-finished ("planning is the boring half") and positions GeKnee as the in-trip half no one else owns. Current A6 V1-V3 already lead with live mode; V4 sharpens the contrast.
