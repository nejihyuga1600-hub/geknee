# Competitor website scrape — 2026-05-28

First-pass landing-page extracts across the 4 segments. Captured: hero headline, hero sub-copy, primary CTA, social proof stats, social handles, one-line positioning.

## Top-line insights

### What's working in the AI-travel-planner category

1. **Speed is the dominant hook.** Everyone leads with time-to-plan:
   - Layla — "Your trip. Planned in minutes."
   - Mindtrip — "Travel better." (chat = "Start chatting")
   - GuideGeek — "Plan complex trips in minutes."
   - iplan.ai — "smart itinerary…in seconds"
   - TripPlanner.ai — "saves you hours planning"
   - GeKnee already plays here ("60 seconds") — competitive.

2. **The "endless tabs" pain framing is shared territory.** TripPlanner.ai's sub-copy is literally "Smarter than endless tabs." GeKnee's slide 1 caption ("47 tabs. Sticky notes…") uses the same frame. We're not outflanked, but it's not a moat either.

3. **Big numbers when you have them.** Hopper "120M travelers." Wanderlog "1M users, 8M trips planned." TripPlanner.ai "8M+ trips planned." These are featured at fold-level. GeKnee has 0 right now — wait to claim until honest, but plan placement for when we cross 10K trips.

4. **Almost no one links Instagram from their website.** Layla, Mindtrip, iplan, GuideGeek surface IG handles; Wonderplan, Wanderlog, TripPlanner.ai, Kayak don't link IG anywhere we found. IG isn't a top-of-funnel growth lever for most AI planner brands — *implication: organic IG is contested less heavily; the marginal benchmark is probably indie creators (Segment 4), not corporate AI planners.*

5. **CTAs are universally short, conversational, and free.** "Get started—it's free", "Plan my trip", "Start planning", "Say hi!", "Start chatting", "Search". GeKnee's "Plan yours free → geknee.com" is correctly in this lane.

### Category-shift signal

- **Roam Around is gone — redirects to layla.ai.** Either acquired, merged, or strategic redirect. Roam Around was *the* viral AI itinerary launch of 2023 — this is a meaningful market consolidation. Watch for Layla absorbing more of the early-AI-planner cohort.
- **Vacay.ai is for sale (parked on atom.com)** — defunct as a product. Removed from active competitor set.

### What GeKnee can differentiate on (not represented in any competitor we scraped)

- **Globe-first UX.** Layla/Mindtrip/Wonderplan all lead with a CHAT or FORM. None lead with a 3D globe. Our slide 4 (real-app/globe) is the strongest differentiator visual we have — consider promoting it to slide 1 in some carousel variants.
- **Monument collection / gamification.** Zero AI planners have a "collect the world" mechanic. The geknee.com home page already leads with "GO THERE. PROVE IT." — this is the wedge a real planner can't copy quickly.
- **Cosmic-editorial aesthetic.** Everyone else is white-background, business-casual UI screenshots. Our dark-cosmic carousel slides occupy aesthetic space no AI planner currently owns.
